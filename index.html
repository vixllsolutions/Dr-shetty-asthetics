DR. M. K. SHETTYDERMATOLOGY · AESTHETIC LASER MEDICINE

Consultant Dermatologist & Aesthetic Laser Physician

Dr. M. K. Shetty

A dermatology-led approach to skin, hair and aesthetic concerns, built around consultation, explanation and an individual treatment plan.

Public professional profiles list MBBS from Mysore Medical College (1986), a dermatology diploma from Bangalore Medical College and Research Institute (1990), MD in Dermatology from Command Hospital, Air Force, Bengaluru (1993), and a fellowship in Dermatologic Laser Surgery at Singapore’s National Skin Centre (2000). With more than three decades in the field, his recorded career also includes teaching at Dr. Ambedkar Medical College, visiting specialist work in the Middle East, and professional training and academic contributions in aesthetic dermatology. Public doctor profiles also list an “Early Pioneer in Aesthetic Dermatology” recognition (2013).
